How to use Infusion Manager

Test prototype only: Use synthetic data, not actual medication preparation or administration. The calculator has not been clinically validated and is not connected to pumps.

1 · Start a session

Give the session a name if you like. Your changes auto-save locally in this browser. Use Save & close to archive a session, Open to resume one, or New session to start separately.

2 · Prepare a syringe

Enter the shared animal weight. Select a drug, verify the stock concentration and enter target dose and preferred mL/hr. Review the proposed 60 mL recipe. Save it as planned or confirm prepared and started. Editing a proposed recipe does not change the active syringe.

3 · Adjust an infusion

Select Change dose / rate on the drug card. Enter a new target dose or pump rate plus physically remaining solution. Review the calculated rate, dose and duration; Record adjustment only after the change is confirmed.

4 · Complete, edit and review

History includes all three drugs, newest first. Complete syringe records 0 mL remaining as an assumption. Tap a history entry to correct remaining volume, document losses, or add a note; corrections retain earlier information. Detailed drug accounting is below the dashboard.

5 · Back up and restore

Export JSON backup saves a dated copy of the current session. Import JSON creates a separate local session from that file. Always verify the download exists. Local storage and imported sessions are device/browser specific. A page closing cannot reliably trigger a download.

About the totals

Estimated drug out is drug calculated to have left the syringe; it is not verified administered to the animal. Planned syringes are excluded, and the last entered remaining volume is a snapshot—not a live pump reading. A one-click completion assumes 0 mL remaining. Tap that syringe to correct the final volume or document priming, discarded solution, or other losses. Prepared, unused, drug out, documented loss, and estimated delivery are different quantities; unknown losses are flagged rather than counted as zero. The detailed accounting table is at the bottom of the page.
